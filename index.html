Recipes

Right-click a workbench or look at it and press E to open its recipe list. Mine redstone ore with an iron pickaxe; craft dust, a lever and a lamp. Place dust and levers on solid blocks, connect dust to a lamp, then right-click or press E on the lever to switch it. Smelt sand into glass, craft empty bottles, use a bottle on water, then purify it at a campfire.
